# Unmind

> Unmind is a workplace mental health platform for employers: therapy, coaching, manager training, crisis care, an Employee Assistance Programme (EAP), wellbeing content, insights and assessments, and Nova, an AI wellbeing coach. There is no public developer API; api.unmind.com serves a private Apollo GraphQL backend used by the Unmind apps.
